## Positioning

Protocolary transforms structured protocol intent into governed, reviewable downstream study
artifacts. The initial product, Protocolary Build, focuses on protocol-to-EDC transformation.

Suggested descriptor:

> Governed clinical study automation

Suggested tagline:

> From protocol intent to reviewable study execution.

## Future Product Family

These names reserve room for expansion without implying that the capabilities already exist:

- **Protocolary Build** — protocol-to-EDC specifications and configurations
- **Protocolary Review** — mapping review, approvals, and governance
- **Protocolary Write** — structured medical-writing workflows
- **Protocolary Impact** — amendment comparison and downstream impact analysis
- **Protocolary Intelligence** — study-design and operational insights

## Usage Rules

- Use **Protocolary** for the brand and platform.
- Use **Protocolary Build** for the current product wedge.
- Keep internal code names functional and brand-neutral where practical.
- Do not use `Transcelerate` as a name for this project or product.
- Preserve **TransCelerate BioPharma**, **TransCelerate DDF**, source URLs, licensing statements,
  and provenance when referring to the independent industry organization or its materials.
- Historical documents may retain the former working name when changing it would distort their
  original context. Add a historical-name note when needed.

## Independence Statement

Protocolary is an independent project. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by
TransCelerate BioPharma Inc. References to TransCelerate DDF identify standards initiatives,
source material, or historical provenance only.
